1. Who is responsible

This notice explains how HikerCircle handles personal data through the mobile app, its website, live-location viewer and connected hiking features. It is designed for use in Malaysia with reference to the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709), as amended, and applicable Malaysian requirements.

Leong Zi Qi, the independent developer of HikerCircle, is the data controller and privacy contact. HikerCircle is operated from Malaysia. Contact leongziqi@gmail.com about how your data is handled or to make a privacy request.

This notice is available in full in Bahasa Melayu and English. Both versions describe the same practices. Reading this notice or continuing to use the app does not, by itself, give permission for every optional use of your data. Device permissions, sharing actions and any additional consent requests apply to the relevant feature.

2. Data we collect and its sources

We collect data that you provide, data generated when you use a feature, information supplied by other users, and information needed from service providers:

  • Account and security: phone number, account and session identifiers, password hashes, verification and recovery records, Authenticator configuration and protected secrets, passkey public credentials, security events and login status. Passkey private keys and fingerprint or face templates used by your device are not sent to HikerCircle.
  • Profile and preferences: username, chosen profile photo, gender, hiking experience and frequency, selected residential area and its coordinates, preferences, buddies, achievements and hiker level.
  • Hiking and location: selected trails, checkpoints, routes, departure and return times, team membership, location coordinates and reported accuracy, capture times, recorded footprints, check-ins, distance, duration, battery status and live-sharing state. Location comes from your phone or from a location you select for a feature.
  • Safety and contacts: emergency-contact names, numbers and relationships that you enter or select; SOS mode, trigger time, selected message and shirt colour; reported fall or stillness events, dispatch outcomes and retry records. A note you choose to provide may reveal health information, which requires additional care.
  • Community and feedback: posts, stories, chat messages, membership and message status, comments, reactions, ratings and difficulty choices, caution reports, support or feedback reports, drafts, and photos, videos or other attachments supported by the feature.
  • Device and nearby context: app/platform details, notification tokens, permission and connectivity state, motion signals used for safety detection, supported SOS button identifiers, local pairing data, nearby observations and optional debugging records. Technical providers may process IP addresses, request times and other connection metadata.

Other users may supply your details when naming you as an emergency contact, inviting you to a hike, or communicating with you. Mapping, weather and messaging providers return the information needed for the requested feature. The app may store records on the phone for offline operation and later synchronize eligible records with its backend.

3. Purposes and required information

We use the data to provide account access and recovery; profiles and preferences; trail discovery, route previews and weather; hike planning, reminders, preparation, tracking and history; team coordination and chat; live sharing, safety checks and SOS attempts; community features; moderation; feedback handling; service security and troubleshooting. We also produce aggregate service totals that do not identify an individual.

Required for the relevant service: a verified phone number and required sign-in/onboarding information are needed for an account. At least one emergency contact is required to finish onboarding. A location-dependent feature needs a usable location, and cloud or messaging operations need connectivity. Without the necessary data, the associated account step or feature cannot be completed.

Optional: a profile photo, community content, additional contacts, attachments and live sharing are optional. Camera, address-book, Bluetooth, background-location and notification access are requested when relevant. You can enter a contact manually instead of importing it. Refusing optional data or permission may limit that feature without authorizing unrelated collection.

HikerCircle does not sell your personal data or use your location, private messages, contacts or SOS details for third-party behavioural advertising.

4. Location recording and sharing links

Location is used for trail context, route and weather requests, active-hike recording, check-ins, route-deviation checks, team coordination, nearby safety features and emergency location. Background access supports enabled tracking or sharing when the app is not visible. A map or weather request can send the selected coordinates to that provider even if public live sharing is off.

Active-hike records are retained locally to support offline use, with eligible updates synchronized when a connection is available. Turning off location permission does not remove records already saved on the phone or in the cloud. The operating system, device or battery settings can interrupt recording.

A live-location link is access to shared information. Anyone who has an active link can open the viewer without a HikerCircle account and can forward it. The shared record can include the latest location, its time and accuracy, and the route made available by that feature. Share it only with people you trust. The viewer does not provide a public directory of all shares.

A new share is valid for 24 hours. An explicit activation or renewal of an active share extends its expiry to 24 hours from that action and can keep the same link. Reopening the app or publishing another location does not automatically renew it. A stopped or expired share receives a new link when activated again.

Stopping sharing stops new phone updates immediately. Viewer access ends when the backend deactivates the share or its expiry is reached; an offline stop request may be retried later. Expiry ends viewer access, not necessarily retention of every related record. It cannot remove screenshots, messages or copies already held by recipients.

5. Emergency contacts, WhatsApp and SOS buttons

When you save another person's contact details, tell them why you are adding them and obtain their agreement to receive the relevant alerts. Entering a number is not a record that its owner has agreed. If you are a named contact and want to raise a concern, use the privacy contact below.

For WhatsApp verification or an SOS/contact notification, the backend passes the recipient number and the message data required for that operation to GREEN-API and WhatsApp. Depending on the operation, this can include a verification message, your name or phone number, incident type and time, selected note, coordinates, a map or live-sharing link and relevant hike information. Saved-contact validation limits SOS dispatch to the account's saved recipients.

The linked-device GREEN-API gateway used by HikerCircle is a third-party integration, separate from Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. A gateway or account restriction can interrupt alerts. The provider handles the recipient number and message supplied to it; do not assume that WhatsApp encryption prevents the gateway or HikerCircle's backend from processing the message submitted for sending. See the GREEN-API explanation and WhatsApp terms.

Eligible failed or offline alerts can be saved for retry after reconnection. Provider acceptance is different from recipient delivery or reading. Stopping a local siren does not cancel a message already accepted for sending. The app does not automatically dispatch official rescue services.

A paired SOS button uses locally saved pairing information. Nearby non-owner button observations may be recorded locally and uploaded as unverified nearby context. Their coordinates can come from a relay or the detecting phone; they do not prove the button's position or authenticate the person carrying it. Nearby observations do not give the observer access to the owner's pairing secret or private account.

6. Who may receive or access data

  • People involved in a feature: the selected emergency contacts, hike hosts and members, buddies, chat recipients, community viewers, report reviewers and holders of an active live-sharing link receive the information the feature makes available. Community content can be copied or reshared.
  • Authorized administrators: permitted account support, trail-request review, moderation, safety administration and feedback review may involve access to the records needed for those tasks. Administrative access is subject to account roles and backend rules.
  • Infrastructure and platform providers: Google Firebase/Google Cloud support authentication, data and file storage, hosting, functions and notifications. Apple services support relevant iOS, notification and passkey operations. These providers may process account identifiers, tokens, uploaded data and technical connection information needed for those services.
  • Map, route and weather providers: depending on the feature, Mapbox, Google Maps/Routes, Geoapify, OpenRouteService, OpenTopoData, OpenStreetMap-based services and Open-Meteo may receive selected coordinates, route points and technical request information.
  • Communication providers: GREEN-API and WhatsApp process the recipient and message information described above. Opening the phone dialler, a map app or the phone's sharing menu also involves the app or service you choose.
  • Legal or safety recipients: information may be disclosed to a competent authority or another appropriate party where required or permitted by law, including a lawful request, a security investigation or protection of vital interests.

Other app users are not given general access to private authentication data, emergency-contact lists or notification tokens. Choosing a public or sharing feature changes the audience for that feature; account authentication does not make an active shared link private to a named recipient.

7. Device permissions and your controls

You control device permissions through Android or iOS settings. Relevant permissions include location, background location, motion, Bluetooth/nearby devices, camera or flashlight, selected photos/files, contacts and notifications.

Address-book access is used when you choose to pick a contact. The selected emergency-contact fields are saved for that purpose; HikerCircle does not upload your entire address book for that action. You can select media rather than give access to unrelated files where the operating system supports it.

Revoking a permission stops or limits the related collection after the change takes effect, but it does not withdraw content already sent or delete cloud records. Device permission is a technical permission and is not blanket consent for every disclosure, overseas transfer or future purpose. Use Stop Live, the relevant feature settings, or contact us if you also wish to stop processing or request deletion.

8. Malaysia PDPA and consent

The Malaysian framework includes the General, Notice and Choice, Disclosure, Security, Retention, Data Integrity and Access Principles. Our data handling must have a lawful purpose and the applicable legal basis. Where consent is required, it must be obtained for that processing. Other permitted grounds can include necessary performance of a contract, a legal obligation or protection of vital interests; an emergency is not blanket permission for unrelated processing.

Where a feature requires consent, we must explain its purpose and choices. You may withdraw consent by writing to the contact below, and use app or device controls for the associated collection. Some requested services cannot continue without the information they require. Receipt of a privacy notice alone is not consent, and changes of purpose may require a new notice and consent.

The post-onboarding notice uses a checkbox that is not preselected and a separate Agree & continue action. An acceptance receipt records the notice and policy versions, chosen language, action time on your phone and server recording time. It is saved on your phone first and uploaded to your account when a connection is available, so the same notice need not be accepted again. Choosing Not now signs you out without recording acceptance; it does not delete information already provided for account setup. Agreeing does not activate optional features or device permissions.

SOS notes or reports may contain health information. Do not include more than is needed. Processing sensitive personal data requires explicit consent or another condition permitted by Malaysian law. Device biometric verification does not mean HikerCircle receives your fingerprint or face template.

9. Processing outside Malaysia

HikerCircle is operated from Malaysia, but cloud, mapping, platform and communication providers may route, process, back up or store data in other countries. Provider locations differ by service and configuration. A Malaysian user or a Malaysia-based developer does not mean every copy remains in Malaysia.

Where section 129 of Act 709 applies, an overseas transfer needs a permitted condition and appropriate protection. Depending on the circumstances, this may involve an adequately protective destination, informed consent, contractual necessity or another statutory condition. Consent is not assumed simply because you use the app. You may contact us for information about the providers, destination information available for the feature, purpose and applicable transfer arrangements. See the Commissioner's 2025 cross-border transfer guidelines.

10. How long records remain

Retention depends on the record and its purpose. We do not treat live-link expiry as a universal data-deletion period.

  • Live viewer: access lasts until deactivation or the current expiry, including an explicit renewal. Related account, hike, route and security records may remain separately.
  • Verification: OTP, recovery and MFA challenges have short validity windows. A challenge becoming invalid does not mean every log or underlying record is deleted at the same moment.
  • Hike history, profiles and content: records are kept while needed for the account, selected history/community features or a request under review. They are reviewed for removal when that purpose ends.
  • Retry and security records: pending operations, dispatch outcomes, abuse-prevention and request logs may remain as needed to reconcile work, investigate an incident or meet a legal requirement. A retry deadline does not automatically erase the alert record.
  • Local and recipient copies: app data and drafts may remain on your phone until removed by a supported feature, clearing app data or uninstalling. Recipients can retain their own copies. Backups and provider logs can follow separate service lifecycles.

We must not keep personal data longer than necessary for its purpose. A deletion request is assessed across the relevant local, cloud and provider-held records; any justified retention or limits will be explained. There is no promise here that all history or backups are erased automatically after 24 hours.

11. Security and personal-data breaches

Safeguards include account authentication, MFA where configured, account-role checks, database/storage rules, protected credential records, server-held messaging credentials, network transport protection and abuse controls. They do not make every record end-to-end encrypted or every local database immune to device access. Protect your phone, screen lock, account and sharing links.

If a personal-data breach occurs, we will investigate, contain it, assess its impact and take appropriate remedial steps. Where notification is required, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and affected individuals according to Malaysian law and the applicable breach-notification guidelines. Use the privacy contact to report suspected unauthorized access. Do not send us a password, verification code, Authenticator secret or passkey private key.

12. Access, correction and other choices

You may contact us to request access to or correction of your personal data, withdraw consent in writing, or exercise an applicable right under Act 709. Where the statutory conditions are met, you may request prevention of processing likely to cause unwarranted substantial damage or distress, prevention of direct marketing, or data portability subject to technical feasibility and compatible formats.

We verify identity using information reasonably necessary for the request and respond within applicable statutory periods. Some requests have lawful exceptions or fees; we will explain a restriction, any applicable fee, or an extension rather than promise an unconditional right. You can also update supported profile fields, contacts, content, notifications and sharing settings in the app. We do not currently send direct marketing.

A request to access or correct data is different from a request to delete an account. Contact us if you are an emergency contact or another person whose details were provided by a user.

13. Account and data deletion requests

Email leongziqi@gmail.com with the subject HikerCircle account or data deletion. Give the account phone number and describe whether you want the account, a particular hike, contact record, report or other data removed. Provide only the information needed to identify the request; we will arrange appropriate verification.

Deletion is handled as a request to the developer, not a claim that an automatic in-app erasure button exists. We will explain the records covered, any information still needed for security or law, and limitations involving shared messages, recipient copies or backups. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not by itself delete the cloud account or recall WhatsApp messages. If an offline device later reconnects, a pending local change may still require reconciliation; tell us if you also need help addressing that copy.

Do not add another child's details without the appropriate authority. Contact us if a child's data has been supplied without the required authorization so it can be investigated and handled appropriately.

Pilihan: foto profil, kandungan komuniti, kenalan tambahan, lampiran dan perkongsian langsung adalah pilihan. HikerCircle tidak menjual data peribadi anda atau menggunakan lokasi, mesej peribadi, kenalan atau butiran SOS anda untuk pengiklanan tingkah laku pihak ketiga.

4. Rakaman lokasi dan pautan perkongsian

Lokasi digunakan bagi konteks denai, permintaan laluan dan cuaca, rakaman pendakian aktif, daftar masuk keselamatan, semakan penyimpangan laluan, penyelarasan pasukan, ciri keselamatan berdekatan dan lokasi kecemasan. Akses latar belakang menyokong penjejakan atau perkongsian yang diaktifkan apabila aplikasi tidak kelihatan. Permintaan peta atau cuaca boleh menghantar koordinat yang dipilih kepada penyedia walaupun perkongsian langsung awam dimatikan.

Rekod pendakian aktif disimpan secara setempat bagi menyokong kegunaan luar talian, dengan kemas kini yang layak disegerakkan apabila sambungan tersedia. Mematikan kebenaran lokasi tidak memadamkan rekod yang telah disimpan pada telefon atau dalam awan. Sistem pengendalian, peranti atau tetapan bateri boleh mengganggu rakaman.

Pautan lokasi langsung memberikan akses kepada maklumat yang dikongsi. Sesiapa yang memiliki pautan aktif boleh membuka paparan tanpa akaun HikerCircle dan boleh memajukannya. Rekod yang dikongsi boleh merangkumi lokasi terkini, masa dan ketepatannya, serta laluan yang disediakan oleh ciri tersebut. Kongsi hanya dengan orang yang dipercayai. Paparan tidak menyediakan direktori awam bagi semua perkongsian.

Perkongsian baharu sah selama 24 jam. Pengaktifan atau pembaharuan perkongsian aktif secara nyata melanjutkan tamat tempohnya kepada 24 jam daripada tindakan tersebut dan boleh mengekalkan pautan yang sama. Membuka semula aplikasi atau menerbitkan lokasi lain tidak memperbaharuinya secara automatik. Perkongsian yang dihentikan atau tamat tempoh menerima pautan baharu apabila diaktifkan semula.

Menghentikan perkongsian menghentikan kemas kini telefon baharu dengan serta-merta. Akses paparan tamat apabila pelayan menyahaktifkan perkongsian atau tamat tempohnya dicapai; permintaan berhenti ketika luar talian mungkin dicuba semula kemudian. Tamat tempoh menamatkan akses paparan, bukan semestinya penyimpanan setiap rekod berkaitan. Ia tidak boleh membuang tangkap layar, mesej atau salinan yang telah dimiliki penerima.

5. Kenalan kecemasan, WhatsApp dan butang SOS

Apabila anda menyimpan butiran kenalan orang lain, maklumkan sebab mereka ditambah dan dapatkan persetujuan mereka untuk menerima amaran yang berkaitan. Memasukkan nombor bukan rekod bahawa pemiliknya telah bersetuju. Jika anda dinamakan sebagai kenalan dan ingin mengemukakan kebimbangan, gunakan maklumat hubungan privasi di bawah.

Bagi pengesahan WhatsApp atau pemberitahuan SOS/kenalan, pelayan menghantar nombor penerima dan data mesej yang diperlukan kepada GREEN-API dan WhatsApp. Mengikut operasi, ini boleh termasuk mesej pengesahan, nama atau nombor telefon anda, jenis dan masa kejadian, nota yang dipilih, koordinat, pautan peta atau perkongsian langsung dan maklumat pendakian berkaitan. Pengesahan kenalan tersimpan mengehadkan penghantaran SOS kepada penerima tersimpan bagi akaun tersebut.

Gerbang peranti terpaut GREEN-API yang digunakan oleh HikerCircle ialah integrasi pihak ketiga yang berasingan daripada WhatsApp Business Platform rasmi Meta. Sekatan gerbang atau akaun boleh mengganggu amaran. Penyedia mengendalikan nombor penerima dan mesej yang dibekalkan; jangan menganggap penyulitan WhatsApp menghalang gerbang atau pelayan HikerCircle daripada memproses mesej yang diserahkan untuk dihantar. Lihat penerangan GREEN-API dan terma WhatsApp.

Amaran gagal atau luar talian yang layak boleh disimpan untuk cubaan semula selepas sambungan pulih. Penerimaan oleh penyedia berbeza daripada penghantaran kepada penerima atau pembacaan mesej.   • Salinan setempat dan penerima: data aplikasi dan draf mungkin kekal pada telefon sehingga dibuang melalui ciri yang disokong, pengosongan data aplikasi atau penyahpasangan.
