Before installing the Android beta
  1. Download the APK on your Android phone and open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow installation from this browser or file manager for this installation. Keep Play Protect enabled; do not bypass a harmful-app warning.
  3. Open HikerCircle and sign in or register. Grant only the permissions needed for the features you choose.
  4. You can turn off that browser or file manager’s installation permission afterwards.

This is pre-release software. SOS actions can contact real people: do not send test emergencies. Delivery depends on connectivity and service availability; HikerCircle is not an emergency service or a guarantee of rescue.

Private-key route-provider fallbacks are excluded from this beta; saved trail routes, public-token Mapbox features and the beta-restricted Google services remain configured. Future store builds may require a separate installation if their signing identity differs. Do not uninstall an existing version without protecting any local-only data.

Read the privacy policy before joining the beta.
