01 / SMARTWATCH

Progress at a glance. Safety within reach.

A proposed watch companion could show hike progress, warnings and SOS confirmation. A compatible GPS watch could also record footprints while reducing the phone's recording workload.

  • Hike progress and warnings on the wrist
  • A deliberate SOS confirmation
  • GPS footprint recording on compatible watches
Explore a scenario · no real messages are sent
A phone link would still matter.

The watch experience would need clear connection and synchronisation status. Seeing an SOS confirmation must not imply that an emergency contact has received a message.

From idea to fieldwork

Before these become trail tools.

01 / BUILD

Prototype and connect

Build compatible watch and radio prototypes, then test how phones, nearby participants and gateways exchange small messages.

02 / PROTECT

Protect and recover

Keep relay information private. Test reconnection, device restarts and queued messages without duplicating confirmed sends.

03 / VALIDATE

Test outdoors

Use supervised hikes and broader user testing to evaluate terrain, detection accuracy, battery endurance and delivery reliability.

A broader direction

More from the recommendations.

The report also identifies improvements to explore across the everyday hiking experience.

Smoother hiking
  • Smoother FPV maps, lower battery use and lighter data exchange.
  • Editable upcoming hikes, with participants informed when a plan changes.
  • A return-to-start option that follows recorded footprints.
More useful safety
  • A maintained emergency-number registry, with relevant rescue contacts.
  • A structured medical profile for allergies, conditions and medication, with a choice of what accompanies each SOS.
  • Emergency-contact invitations and acceptance before enrolment.
  • Two-way acknowledgements showing which contact is responding.
More control and connection
  • Malay, Chinese and Tamil language support.
  • AI-assisted community moderation and carefully designed end-to-end chat encryption.
  • User-chosen sharing durations and private sharing history.
  • Comprehensive account-data erasure, beyond closing the account.
  • Migration to the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

These are research concepts, not features available in the beta. Compatibility, range and delivery need field validation. No release date, coverage or rescue guarantee.

One circle, the whole journey

From the first plan to the final check-in.

HikerCircle keeps the useful details together so your group can spend less time coordinating and more time moving with confidence.

01 · BEFORE

Choose and plan

Discover trails, review essential details, invite your hiking buddies, and agree on the plan before anyone leaves home.

02 · DURING

Move as a circle

Track hike progress, share location when needed, check in, and keep everyone connected through one shared hike.

03 · AFTER

Keep the story

Finish with a useful hike summary, route history, memories, and trail feedback that helps the next adventure.

What HikerCircle does

The essentials, without the trail clutter.

01

Trail discovery

Browse trails with useful location, distance, difficulty, route, and weather context in one place.

02

Shared hike plans

Set the route, timing, checkpoints, and team so everyone starts with the same expectations.

03

Buddy circles

Invite trusted hikers, form a team, and keep group conversations attached to the hike they belong to.

04

Live progress

Record the route, place manual pins, share live location intentionally, and check in along the way.

05

SOS and safety tools

Access manual SOS options, emergency-contact delivery, nearby alerts, and supported paired-button features.

06

Hike history

See completed hikes, route summaries, achievements, ratings, and the progress behind every adventure.

Designed with care

Safety should travel with the group.

HikerCircle helps hikers share the right information with the right people when it matters—without making every hike feel like an emergency.

  • ✓Live location is shared intentionally and can be stopped.
  • ✓Emergency contacts and nearby alerts support faster awareness.
  • ✓Fall-and-stillness monitoring can begin a cancellable SOS countdown on supported devices.
HikerCircle supports preparedness and situational awareness. It is not an emergency service, medical device, or guarantee of rescue.
